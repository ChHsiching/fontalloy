# Normalization: the auto school (grid scale capped by the optical ratio)

Donor CJK glyphs scale by k = min(school term, 0.95·(typoAsc + |typoDesc|)/H_ink), where the school term is the grid term 2A/U (CJK advance = exactly 2× the Latin advance — the Maple Mono CN / Sarasa terminal convention), the optical term 1.23·cap/H_ink (Han ink ≈ 1.23× cap height, the Noto native pairing — measured: Noto Sans SC's own Han ink p90 906 over its Latin cap 733 = 1.236), or — the default — the smaller of the two. The optical school is an adjustable knob in the recipe and the interface. Vertical displacement is zero: scripts share one baseline, the CJK ideographic em box sits at [−0.12em, +0.88em] by convention (the OpenType baseline-tags spec's registered example: Kozuka Mincho's −120/+880 at 1000 upem), and no top alignment happens. Line height fully inherits the base font (hhea/typo untouched); only usWinAscent/Descent grow, and only to prevent clipping. Donor advances follow UAX #11 in every school: Fullwidth/Wide take two cells, Halfwidth/Narrow and Ambiguous take one — an ambiguous codepoint the base font already covers stays with the base font, a corollary of absence-never-overrides. Width policy for fullwidth quotes and dashes is a recipe option.

## Round-20 correction: the default was the bare grid, and it overshot the standard

The original title of this ADR was "Normalization defaults to the grid school; optical ratio is the knob", and the rationale was "in a terminal the 2:1 grid is correctness, not taste". The round-20 verdict (owner item 7: "the synthesized font shows CJK visibly taller than Latin") measured the default against the standard and found it out of range:

- JetBrainsMono (Nerd Font Regular: cap 730, A 600, typo 1020/−300) × LXGW WenKai Mono (Regular: H_ink p90 921 of a 1000 upem) under the bare grid term k = 2A/U = 1.2 put the merged font's Han ink at 1106 units = **1.515× the Latin cap height** — far above the Noto-school zone (~1.05–1.3×; the interface's 自动 promised "按两款字体自带的尺寸数据自动选择" but the GUI mapping silently resolved auto to the unclamped grid term).
- The same pair under the auto school: k = min(1.2, 1.23·730/921 = 0.975) = 0.975, Han ink 898 = **1.230×** the cap height — the Noto native pairing's own number. Han bottom stays at −101, inside the ideographic em box's −120 bottom; zero vertical-metric table changes either way.

The fix keeps everything else in this ADR unchanged: advances stay on the UAX #11 grid whatever the school (the scale and the advance are orthogonal — the optical school already shipped glyphs narrower than their two-cell advance), zero vertical displacement, line height inherited, the guard respected. The bare grid school remains an explicit choice (the recipe's `mode: grid`, the interface's 底边一样高 — for terminal-purist builds that want the ink to fill the em the advance bought), and the pinned official recipes keep their explicit `mode: grid` so their published report artifacts stay reproducible.
